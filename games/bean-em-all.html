About the game

Begin with no skills or controls and slowly earn abilities in a fast-paced bullet-mayhem game starring the baddest bean.

Build the ultimate moveset as you progress. Chain attacks between adjacent enemies to trigger Bean-Reactions, gain bullets, and survive a tiny but dangerous kitchen world.

Features

What to expect

  • Progression that starts with almost no controls
  • Chain-reaction combat that rewards positioning
  • Fast precision shooting and dashing
  • Created for the WTF x IGDC Game Jam 2024

Controls

How to play

  • WASD — Move
  • Hold Left Mouse — Aim; release to shoot
  • Space — Dash
  • Attack adjacent enemies to trigger a Bean-Reaction
