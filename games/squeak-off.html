About the game

Blast through waves of annoying squeaky toys to defeat their leader and silence the chaos once and for all.

Max, a retired veteran, spends his quiet days in a shelter surrounded by kids and laughter. One day, the endlessly squeaking toys push him too far, and he sets out to take down their mechanized leader.
