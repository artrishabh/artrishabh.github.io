# Cozy theme guide

The new design is an override layer, so the existing layout and content remain intact.

## Files
- `cozy-theme.css`: portfolio colors and cartoon-style components
- `cozy-theme.js`: palette selection and saved preference
- `games/cozy-game-theme.css`: game catalogue and project-page styling

## Included palettes
1. **Sunlit Workshop** (`sunny`) — sky blue, honey yellow, coral, and cream. Default.
2. **Berry Picnic** (`berry`) — raspberry, mint, peach, and blush.
3. **Forest Camp** (`forest`) — moss green, marmalade, parchment, and muted violet.

Visitors can switch palettes from the desktop sidebar. Their choice is saved in the browser and also applies to the game catalogue and project pages.
